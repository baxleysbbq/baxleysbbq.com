Privacy Policy — Baxleys BBQ Sandwiches

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Information we collect

When you sign up for our list, place an order, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your first name
  • Your email address
  • Your mobile phone number, if you choose to receive text messages
  • Information you provide about market days, orders, or preferences

We also collect basic information automatically when you visit our website, such as pages visited and general device and browser information, through our website hosting and analytics tools.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Send you emails and text messages about market days, menus, and specials, only if you have opted in
  • Respond to your questions and orders
  • Operate and improve our website, markets, and service
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages (SMS)

If you opt in to receive text messages from Baxleys BBQ Sandwiches, you agree to receive marketing text messages about market days, menus, and specials.

  • Message frequency may vary.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.
  • To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any text message. To get help, reply HELP, or contact us at info@baxleysbbq.com.

Mobile information and consent: No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We use a messaging service provider to deliver text messages on our behalf. That provider may process your phone number and message content solely to deliver the messages you requested.

Email

If you opt in to receive email from us, we will use your email address to send market updates, menus, and specials. You may unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by contacting us at info@baxleysbbq.com.

Sharing your information

We may share your information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate our business, such as our email and text messaging provider, solely to provide the services you requested
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property
  • In connection with a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of the business

We do not share your mobile information or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Your choices

  • Email: Unsubscribe using the link in any email, or email info@baxleysbbq.com.
  • Text messages: Reply STOP to any text message to opt out.
  • Contact us: Email info@baxleysbbq.com to ask about, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. We retain your information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

Children

Our website and lists are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@baxleysbbq.com or at the address above.
